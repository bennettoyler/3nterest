# Model licences

Every file in this folder is a derivative of a CC0 (public domain) source. CC0 needs no attribution; authors are credited anyway.

All files were converted from glTF (Poly Haven, 1k textures) or OBJ (Kenney) to USDZ, rescaled per axis to the product's dimensions in `convex/seedData.ts` (metres, Y up, origin at the floor centre, front facing +Z), and textures re-encoded as JPEG at most 1024 px. Extra rotations are listed below.
